# VioletWay

### Contributors: [Sufyan Waryah](https://github.com/sufyanw) & [Alisha Zaman](https://github.com/az2924)

## What and Why?
VioletWay is a mobile, web-based carpooling and buddy-system application designed specifically for New York University (NYU) students commuting in and out of the New York City campus. Often, students commute from campus late at night, whether after evening lectures, lab sessions, or late-night study sessions, which poses significant safety concerns and financial stress for students who then rely on either expensive rideshare applications, such as Uber or Lyft, or public transportation. VioletWay resolves this issue by connecting verified NYU students who are traveling along similar routes during both peak and off-peak commuting times. Through this, VioletWay creates a trusted peer network that increases the students’ physical and emotional safety through strength in sheer numbers, reduction in individual transportation costs, and an overall stronger campus community. This issue must be addressed, as a student’s well-being and academic success are dependent on safe and affordable transportation on campus.

## For whom?
This app is designed for NYU students, with an emphasis on commuter students. While NYU students do have access to apps like Passio GO for buses and NYU Safe ride for late night rides to dorms, these apps are designed for inter-campus transportation, whereas VioletWay is for commuters who would like to make more friends and save on their commute, given the lack of time they have in-person. This app is also useful for female NYU students, who may not feel comfortable travelling alone at night due to safety concerns.

## How?
Initially, users register with their NYU email address, which grants them access to the application. They set up their profile with their gender, email, default route, and select role option(s), such as walking buddy, ridesharer, driver, etc.
To specify journeys, they can use filters, such as estimated departure times and covered drop-off zones. Users who are drivers can post their routes with the number of available seats, scheduled departure times, and destination.
To manage booking and coordination, users can email that specific student, as their email is available, and go from there.

## Scope?
The delivery of this app is possible to build in a single semester with a team of 4-6 students, given we narrow it down to the following:
1. Users can sign-up using OAuth (NYU emails only)
2. Users fill out a form upon sign-up, where they can indicate their gender, starting location (general, i.e. a specific train/bus station, rather than their home address), the days of the week they commute, and which campus they go to (Brooklyn/Washington Square). Mode of transportation would also be in this form: subway, LIRR, NJT, Coach Bus, car, bicycle, mix of both, etc. We will give users the option to change their commute after signing up, given anything changes.
3. After users enter their information, they can find others who have similar commutes and display their emails. If they decide that they’re interested in commuting with that other student, they can send them an email, and go from there!
